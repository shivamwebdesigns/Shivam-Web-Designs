Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our packages, billing, and deliverables.

What does each package price include?+

Design, custom development, responsive tuning, domain/subdomain connection, and the dedicated post-launch support period listed. There are zero hidden fees.

Can I order just add-ons if I already have a website?+

Yes, absolutely! You can choose any add-ons (like Logo Design for ₹99, Copywriting, or Subdomain Setup) without selecting a package. Just paste your website link in the cart box so we can review your current site.

How does payment and milestone billing work?+

Starter is paid upon kickoff. Growth and Pro follow a 50% upfront commitment and 50% final settlement prior to production DNS launch. Final source code and credentials are transferred upon full clearance.

How do revision cycles work?+

Every package includes 3 structured revision rounds free of charge. A round consists of your consolidated feedback items. Extra rounds can be added for ₹199.

Do I retain 100% ownership of my website?+

Yes. Upon final settlement, all custom frontend source code, design assets, and content are assigned 100% to you without restrictive vendor lock-in.

What content do I need to provide?+

Please share your logo, photos, and basic text. If you need assistance with brand writing or logo creation, our add-ons start at just ₹99.

I need something custom or enterprise. What now?+

For custom web applications or multi-page flagships, visit our Start a Project page to submit a tailored brief.
