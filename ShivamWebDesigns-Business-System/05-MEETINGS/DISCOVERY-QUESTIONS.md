# ShivamWebDesigns — Strategic Discovery Questions

Use these 20 diagnostic questions during client discovery calls to uncover high-value opportunities, understand brand psychology, and eliminate scope ambiguity.

---

## Category A: Business & Commercial Goals
1. *What is the primary commercial purpose of this website redesign or build? (e.g., Direct sales, lead generation, enterprise investor credibility)*
2. *If we were to look back 6 months from launch, what specific metric or outcome would tell you this project was an overwhelming success?*
3. *What is your current monthly traffic, and what percentage of that traffic currently converts into inquiries or sales?*
4. *What is the average lifetime value (LTV) of a single new customer or client for your business?*
5. *Are there any critical business milestones or product launches driving the target deadline for this site?*

---

## Category B: Target Audience & Customer Psychology
6. *Who is your ideal customer profile (ICP)? Describe their role, priorities, and main frustrations.*
7. *When an ideal prospect lands on your homepage, what is the single most important thought or feeling you want them to have within the first 5 seconds?*
8. *What are the most common objections or hesitations prospects have before purchasing or reaching out to you?*
9. *How will your target audience primarily access the site — desktop workstations, mobile phones on the go, or tablets?*

---

## Category C: Current Website Audit & Technical Pain Points
10. *What frustrates you the most about your current website today?*
11. *Have clients or prospects ever complained about slow loading speeds, confusing navigation, or mobile layout issues?*
12. *What third-party tools, CRMs, email software, or analytics must seamlessly integrate with the new site?*
13. *Do you have internal team members who need to update blog posts or case studies, or do you prefer our studio to handle ongoing maintenance?*

---

## Category D: Aesthetic Direction & Competitive Landscape
14. *Who are your top 2–3 direct competitors, and how do you want your digital experience to decisively outperform theirs?*
15. *Can you share 2–3 websites (even outside your industry) whose aesthetic, typography, or interactions you deeply admire?*
16. *Are there any specific visual elements, color schemes, or clichés in your industry that you want to strictly avoid?*
17. *Do you have finalized high-resolution photography, brand guidelines, and copy, or will you need guidance structuring content?*

---

## Category E: Scope, Budget & Decision Making
18. *Who on your team has final written sign-off authority on design and scope approvals?*
19. *Have you established a formal budget investment range for this initiative ($1,000–$2,500, $2,500–$5,000, or $5,000+)?*
20. *What is your ideal kickoff date once the proposal and agreement are finalized?*
