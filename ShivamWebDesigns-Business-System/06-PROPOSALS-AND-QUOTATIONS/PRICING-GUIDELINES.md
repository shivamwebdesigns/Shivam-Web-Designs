# ShivamWebDesigns — Studio Pricing Guidelines & Commercial Strategy

> [!NOTE]
> This is an internal strategy guide. Do not send this document to clients. Client-facing proposals must use `PROJECT-PROPOSAL.md` and `QUOTATION-TEMPLATE.md`.

---

## 1. Commercial Philosophy: Value-Based vs. Hourly Billing

ShivamWebDesigns **never bills by the hour**. Hourly billing penalizes engineering efficiency and misaligns agency incentives with client outcomes. 

We quote **fixed-scope, milestone-based sprint pricing**:
- Clients receive complete price certainty with zero hidden fees.
- The studio is rewarded for high velocity, design excellence, and clean code.

---

## 2. Indicative Baseline Service Tiers (Editable Benchmarks)

Use these baseline investment ranges when scoping new projects. Adjust based on company size, revenue impact, and custom feature complexity.

### Tier 1: Sprint Flagship (Estimated 2–3 Weeks)
- **Scope:** 1–3 pages (High-impact flagship landing page, about/story, inquiry form).
- **Ideal For:** Personal brands, high-growth startups, boutique creators, single-product flagships.
- **Indicative Range:** `$1,000 – $2,500` (India baseline: `₹80,000 – ₹2,00,000`).

### Tier 2: Comprehensive Studio Build (Estimated 4–6 Weeks)
- **Scope:** 4–7 bespoke pages (Home, About, Services Suite, Filterable Case Studies, Contact, Legal Hub).
- **Includes:** Full custom Figma art direction, Next.js 15 clean architecture, custom lead configurators, 100/100 Core Web Vitals.
- **Ideal For:** Established businesses, boutique consultancies, agencies, luxury e-commerce showcases.
- **Indicative Range:** `$2,500 – $5,000` (India baseline: `₹2,00,000 – ₹4,00,000`).

### Tier 3: Enterprise & Spatial Interactive (Estimated 6–10 Weeks)
- **Scope:** 8+ pages, custom 3D WebGL / Canvas interactive simulations, complex API integrations, multi-language routing.
- **Ideal For:** Funded tech companies, venture firms, high-end consumer brands.
- **Indicative Range:** `$5,000 – $10,000+` (India baseline: `₹4,00,000 – ₹8,00,000+`).
