### 4. Scope Assessment Notice
- **Within-Scope Adjustments:** Text edits, spacing refinements, color adjustments, image replacements, and layout tuning are standard within-scope revisions.
- **Out-of-Scope Requests:** Requests for entirely new pages, unquoted third-party software integrations, complex database setups, or complete conceptual shifts after wireframe approval require a separate change order and quotation.
