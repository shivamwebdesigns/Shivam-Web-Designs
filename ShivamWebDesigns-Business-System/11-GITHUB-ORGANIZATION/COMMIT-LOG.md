# Master System Commit & Architecture Log

This document records the foundational architecture and implementation history of the **ShivamWebDesigns Business Operating System**.

---

## Implementation Chronology

### Milestone 1: System Scaffolding & Brand System
- **Directories Scaffolding:** Created 13 operational folders (Modules `00` through `12`).
- **Brand System:** Established monochromatic Swiss design guidelines, typographic scale, agency profile, and vector asset library.
- **PDF Generation:** Rendered branded vector `AGENCY-PROFILE.pdf` via Chrome headless print engine.

### Milestone 2: Client Acquisition & Strategic Outreach
- **Outreach Engineering:** Developed multi-channel cold messaging systems for LinkedIn, Instagram, and Email.
- **Ethical Standards:** Formulated strict anti-spam rules and high-conversion follow-up cadences.
- **Client Research:** Built the 15-point audit checklist and 15-column structured `LEAD-TRACKER-TEMPLATE.csv`.

### Milestone 3: Google Workspace Operations & Client Welcome Kit
- **Inquiry & Onboarding Forms:** Authored structured 13-question intake brief and onboarding asset collection blueprint.
- **Google Sheets CRM:** Specified relational schemas for Leads, Pipeline, Projects, and Retainers.
- **Client Welcome Kit:** Delivered professional welcome letter, studio intro, 5-stage project workflow, and communication guidelines; compiled into `WELCOME-KIT.pdf`.

### Milestone 4: Client Meetings & Proposal Systems
- **Meeting Protocol:** Authored 25-minute client discovery agenda, 20 diagnostic questions, notes template, and follow-up email.
- **Commercial Proposals:** Formulated comprehensive Project Proposal template, itemized Quotation template, and transparent pricing tier guidelines.
- **PDF Generation:** Rendered `PROJECT-PROPOSAL.pdf` and `QUOTATION-TEMPLATE.pdf`.

### Milestone 5: Legal Framework, Statutory Notice & Finance
- **Legal Protection:** Drafted Master Services Agreement, Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA), Studio Terms & Conditions, and Legal Review Checklist.
- **Statutory Notice:** Included mandatory advisory clause highlighting adult legal representative / guardian co-signatory requirement for minor founder contracts.
- **Finance Engine:** Standardized milestone invoices, payment receipts, structured CSV payment tracker, and payment collection SOP.

### Milestone 6: Project Management, Handover & Studio Operations
- **Project Governance:** Deployed `PROJECT-TRACKER.csv`, weekly client status brief, structured revision request form, and milestone sign-off certificate.
- **Handover Runbook:** Created comprehensive website handover guide, 35-point pre-flight launch QA checklist, post-launch maintenance guide, and testimonial survey.
- **GitHub Org & Operations:** Published public `.github` profile README, metadata descriptions, repository settings, daily workflow schedules, client handling SOPs, and file naming rules.
