### 1. Purpose
The Parties desire to enter into discussions regarding prospective web development, UI/UX architecture, digital design, or commercial consulting engagements (the "Purpose"). In connection with this Purpose, each Party may disclose to the other confidential proprietary business, technical, or financial information.

---

### 2. Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party ("Disclosing Party") to the other ("Receiving Party"), whether orally, visually, electronically, or in writing, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure, including but not limited to:
- Source code, software designs, wireframes, technical architectures, and algorithms.
- Business strategies, customer lists, marketing roadmaps, financial projections, and pricing models.
- Unreleased brand assets, creative concepts, unpublished project briefs, and proprietary assets.

---

### 3. Exclusions from Confidential Information
Confidential Information does not include information that:
1. Is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement by Receiving Party.
2. Was already known to Receiving Party without confidentiality restrictions prior to disclosure.
3. Is independently developed by Receiving Party without reference to or reliance on Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.
4. Is rightfully received from a third party without duty of confidentiality.

---

### 4. Obligations of Receiving Party
4.1 **Standard of Care:** The Receiving Party agrees to protect the confidentiality of Disclosing Party's Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own sensitive data, but no less than reasonable professional care.  
4.2 **Zero Exposure Policy:** The Receiving Party will not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent from Disclosing Party.  
4.3 **Internal Need-to-Know:** Access is strictly restricted to employees, legal advisors, and vetted subcontractors who require access for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality terms at least as restrictive as those herein.  

---

### 5. Term & Survival
This Agreement and the duty of confidentiality shall govern all disclosures made for a period of **two (2) years** from the Effective Date.

---

### 6. Return or Destruction of Materials
Upon written request by Disclosing Party or upon termination of discussions, Receiving Party shall promptly return or certify the permanent deletion of all physical and digital copies containing Confidential Information, excluding archival backups maintained in accordance with standard disaster-recovery retention schedules.
