### Recitals
WHEREAS, Client wishes to retain the Agency to perform specialized digital services including UI/UX design, custom frontend web development, performance optimization, and digital experience architecture; and  
WHEREAS, Agency possesses the technical and artistic skill to deliver such services in accordance with agreed specifications;  

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties mutually agree to the following terms:

---

### Section 1: Scope of Work & Deliverables
1.1 **Statement of Work (SOW):** The specific scope, technical deliverables, milestones, and deadlines shall be defined in an attached Statement of Work or official Project Proposal approved in writing by both Parties.  
1.2 **Exclusions & Scope Creep:** Any feature, page, integration, or custom function not explicitly itemized in the approved SOW is deemed out-of-scope and shall require a formal written Revision Request or supplementary quotation before execution.  

---

### Section 2: Revision Cycles & Client Review
2.1 **Allocated Revisions:** Unless otherwise stated in the SOW, each project phase includes up to **two (2) structured review cycles**.  
2.2 **Review Period:** Client shall review deliverables and submit actionable, itemized feedback within **five (5) business days** of receiving a preview link or milestone delivery.  
2.3 **Deemed Approval:** If Client provides no written feedback or approval within **seven (7) business days** of delivery notification, the milestone shall be deemed approved and accepted.  

---

### Section 3: Financial Terms & Invoicing
3.1 **Fee Structure:** Project fees shall follow the milestone schedule defined in the SOW/Quotation (standard: 50% upfront deposit before kickoff, 25% upon design approval, 25% prior to final production code handover and DNS cutover).  
3.2 **Invoices:** Invoices are payable upon receipt or within seven (7) business days via authorized electronic transfer (NEFT/IMPS/UPI or wire transfer).  
3.3 **Late Payments & Suspension:** Agency reserves the right to halt development or withhold staging access if any milestone payment remains overdue past seven (7) calendar days.  

---

### Section 4: Intellectual Property & Rights Transfer
4.1 **Reservation of Rights:** All creative assets, custom source code, layouts, and assets produced during the engagement remain the exclusive intellectual property of ShivamWebDesigns until **all fees, invoices, and applicable taxes are paid in full**.  
4.2 **IP Transfer Upon Full Settlement:** Upon receipt of final payment settlement, Agency assigns to Client all rights, title, and interest in and to the custom front-end code and design assets explicitly created for the project, excluding Agency pre-existing tools, open-source libraries, and reusable utility scripts.  
4.3 **Agency Portfolio Rights:** Agency retains the non-exclusive, perpetual right to display project screenshots, live website URLs, design case studies, and creative credits on `https://shivamwebdesigns.devs.li` and official social channels for marketing and portfolio purposes, unless restricted by a prior non-disclosure agreement.  

---

### Section 5: Client Responsibilities & Materials
5.1 **Assets & Content:** Client is solely responsible for supplying all text copy, brand logos, high-resolution imagery, and licensed assets in a timely manner.  
5.2 **Warranty of Non-Infringement:** Client warrants that all materials provided do not infringe third-party copyrights, trademarks, or proprietary rights, and indemnifies Agency against any third-party claims arising from client-supplied assets.  

---

### Section 6: Warranties & Bug-Fix Period
6.1 **Performance & Code Quality:** Agency warrants that the deliverables will conform to the agreed technical specifications and modern browser standards (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) upon handover.  
6.2 **14-Day Bug Fix Guarantee:** Agency provides a fourteen (14) calendar-day post-launch warranty period during which any objective layout breaks, syntax bugs, or functional discrepancies directly attributable to Agency’s code will be corrected at no additional charge.  
6.3 **Warranty Exclusions:** Warranty does not cover issues resulting from client modifications, third-party plugin updates, hosting server outages, domain DNS misconfigurations, or browser deprecations occurring after launch.  

---

### Section 7: Limitation of Liability
7.1 **Cap on Liability:** In no event shall either party’s aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement exceed the total fees actually paid by Client to Agency under the applicable SOW.  
7.2 **Consequential Damages:** Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, special, punitive, or consequential damages, including loss of business, profits, data, or reputation.  

---

### Section 8: Termination & Cancellation
8.1 **Termination for Convenience:** Either party may terminate this Agreement upon seven (7) business days written notice.  
8.2 **Settlement upon Termination:** If terminated by Client prior to completion, Agency shall retain the non-refundable deposit. Client shall compensate Agency for all completed hours and milestones delivered up to the date of termination. Agency will deliver completed work assets matching settled payments.
