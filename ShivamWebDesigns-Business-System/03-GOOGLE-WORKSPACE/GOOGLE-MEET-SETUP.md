# ShivamWebDesigns — Google Meet Setup & Meeting Protocol

Use this standard operating procedure to prepare, run, and follow up on client discovery calls and design reviews via **Google Meet**.

---

## 1. Pre-Meeting Technical Check (10 Minutes Before)

- [ ] **Google Account:** Ensure you are signed in as `shivamwebdesigns23@gmail.com` (not a personal account).
- [ ] **Meeting Room Link:** Verify the Google Meet link generated on the Google Calendar invite:
  - Form: `https://meet.google.com/xxx-xxxx-xxx`
- [ ] **Audio & Microphone:** Test external microphone. Audio must be crisp with zero background hiss. Enable Google Meet **Noise Cancellation**.
- [ ] **Lighting & Camera:** Frame camera at eye level. Soft front-facing light. Background should be clean, organized, or use a subtle dark studio background.
- [ ] **Screen Share Staging:**
  - Close private tabs, personal WhatsApp, bank records, and email inboxes.
  - Open relevant showcase portfolio tabs:
    - [https://shivamwebdesigns.devs.li](https://shivamwebdesigns.devs.li)
    - [https://shivamwebdesigns.devs.li/businesscard](https://shivamwebdesigns.devs.li/businesscard)
  - Open client's current website and 2–3 visual reference benchmarks.

---

## 2. In-Call Flow & Time Management (25 Minutes)

- **00:00 – 03:00 | Warm Welcome & Rapport:** Thank them for taking the time; confirm call duration and objective.
- **03:00 – 12:00 | Client Discovery & Diagnosis:** Ask open-ended questions from [`05-MEETINGS/DISCOVERY-QUESTIONS.md`](../05-MEETINGS/DISCOVERY-QUESTIONS.md). Listen 70% of the time.
- **12:00 – 18:00 | Strategic Observations & Portfolio Walkthrough:** Share screen; briefly demonstrate how SWD solves similar problems with clean code and sub-second loading.
- **18:00 – 23:00 | Scope Alignment, Timeline & Budget Ranges:** Agree on core deliverables and sprint milestones.
- **23:00 – 25:00 | Next Steps & Mutual Action Items:** Promise written proposal delivery within 24–48 hours.

---

## 3. Post-Meeting Protocol (Within 3 Hours)

1. Review and clean meeting notes using [`05-MEETINGS/MEETING-NOTES-TEMPLATE.md`](../05-MEETINGS/MEETING-NOTES-TEMPLATE.md).
2. Send the formal follow-up email from [`05-MEETINGS/MEETING-FOLLOW-UP.md`](../05-MEETINGS/MEETING-FOLLOW-UP.md) summarizing the discussed scope.
3. Update the deal stage in `03-GOOGLE-WORKSPACE/GOOGLE-SHEETS-CRM.md`.
