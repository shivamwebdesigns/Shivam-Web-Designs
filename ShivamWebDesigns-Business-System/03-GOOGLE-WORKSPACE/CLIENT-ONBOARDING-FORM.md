# ShivamWebDesigns — Client Onboarding Form Blueprint

Send this form to the client immediately after contract execution and deposit receipt. It gathers the raw assets and access credentials required to begin sprint design.

---

## Form Title: Client Project Onboarding & Asset Handover — ShivamWebDesigns
**Form Description:**  
*Welcome to ShivamWebDesigns! To ensure a seamless kickoff and avoid timeline delays, please provide your brand assets, content, and technical access details below.*

---

### Section 1: Project Information & Lead Stakeholders
1. **Project Name & Project ID** (Short answer, Required)
2. **Primary Point of Contact (Name & Title)** (Short answer, Required)
3. **Emergency / Secondary Contact (Name & Email)** (Short answer, Required)
4. **Who holds final written approval authority on design deliverables?** (Short answer, Required)

---

### Section 2: Brand Identity Assets
5. **Brand Guidelines Document (PDF/Figma)** (File upload: PDF, AI, FIG, max 100MB)
6. **Official Vector Logos (SVG, EPS, Transparent PNG)** (File upload, max 100MB)
   - Help text: *Include primary mark, dark mode variant, and favicon/monogram.*
7. **Brand Fonts & Typography Files (TTF/WOFF2 or Google Font names)** (Short answer, Required)
8. **Approved Brand Color Hex Codes** (Paragraph, Required)

---

### Section 3: Content & Media Assets
9. **Shared Drive Link (Google Drive / Dropbox / OneDrive)** (Short answer, Required)
   - Help text: *Please ensure permissions are set to 'Anyone with the link can edit'. Include high-resolution photography, team portraits, product mockups, and written copy.*
10. **Written Website Copy Status** (Multiple choice, Required)
    - ( ) All copy is finalized and ready in the shared drive.
    - ( ) Copy is in draft form and will be updated during the design phase.
    - ( ) Requesting ShivamWebDesigns content structuring / editorial assistance.

---

### Section 4: Domain & Technical Access
11. **Do you currently own your target domain name?** (Multiple choice, Required)
    - ( ) Yes (Registrar: GoDaddy, Namecheap, Google Domains, Cloudflare, etc.)
    - ( ) No (We need recommendations on domain procurement)
12. **DNS Management Access Method** (Multiple choice, Required)
    - ( ) We will invite `shivamwebdesigns23@gmail.com` as a delegated manager in our registrar.
    - ( ) We will configure the A/CNAME records internally based on SWD launch instructions.
13. **Third-Party Services to Integrate** (Checkboxes, Optional)
    - [ ] Google Analytics 4 / Google Tag Manager
    - [ ] Resend / SendGrid / Mailchimp
    - [ ] Stripe / PayPal / Razorpay
    - [ ] Custom CRM / Webhooks
