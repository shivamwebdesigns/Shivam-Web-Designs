# ShivamWebDesigns — Pre-Outreach Client Research Checklist

Perform this 15-point audit on every potential lead *before* composing an outreach message. Record findings in `LEAD-TRACKER-TEMPLATE.csv`.

---

## 1. Business Basics
- [ ] **Business Name:** Verified legal or public trading name.
- [ ] **Contact Person:** Founder, CEO, CMO, or Creative Director identified on LinkedIn / About page.
- [ ] **Active Status:** Confirmed recent social media posts, news, or blog updates within the last 60 days.
- [ ] **Current Website URL:** Reachable over HTTPS (verify SSL certificate).

---

## 2. Visual & Aesthetic Audit
- [ ] **Design Currency:** Does the website look modern, or does it resemble an outdated 2012–2018 template?
- [ ] **Typography & Hierarchy:** Are fonts paired thoughtfully, or are there disjointed sizes, bad line heights, and poor contrast?
- [ ] **Asset Quality:** Are logos pixelated or banners stretched/distorted?
- [ ] **White Space & Spacing:** Is the layout cramped, cluttered, or overwhelmed with generic stock imagery?

---

## 3. Mobile Viewport & Responsiveness (Crucial)
- [ ] **Horizontal Overflow:** Does the mobile layout require sideways scrolling?
- [ ] **Navigation Usability:** Does the hamburger menu open smoothly and cleanly?
- [ ] **Tap Target Sizing:** Are buttons at least 44x44px, or are links too close together to tap comfortably?
- [ ] **Mobile CTA Visibility:** Is the primary call to action immediately visible or buried 10 scrolls down?

---

## 4. Performance & Conversion Flow
- [ ] **Initial Page Load:** Does the site take longer than 2.5–3 seconds to render on a standard mobile connection?
- [ ] **Clear Value Proposition:** Can a first-time visitor understand what the company sells in under 5 seconds?
- [ ] **Inquiry / Booking Friction:** Is there a working contact form, or are visitors forced into an empty `mailto:` link?

---

## 5. Lead Qualification Scorecard
- **Tier A (High Priority):** Established, revenue-generating business with active customers, but an outdated or mobile-broken website holding them back. Clear decision-maker contact available.
- **Tier B (Moderate Priority):** Good product/service with decent web presence, but lacking high-end polish, speed optimization, or modern conversion funnels.
- **Tier C (Low Priority):** Inactive business, completely missing contact details, very early idea stage without budget, or already redesigned with a modern custom tech stack.
