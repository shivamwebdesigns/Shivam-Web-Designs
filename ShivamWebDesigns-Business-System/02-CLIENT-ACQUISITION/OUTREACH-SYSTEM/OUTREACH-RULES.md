# ShivamWebDesigns — Outreach Code of Conduct & Ethical Rules

To maintain our standing as an elite, ethical, and respected web design agency, everyone representing **ShivamWebDesigns** must follow these rules without exception.

---

## 1. Golden Rules of Outreach

1. **Never Lie or Manufacture Evidence:** Never claim a website has malware, is broken, has bad SEO, or is violating laws unless you have concrete, indisputable evidence.
2. **Never Use False Scarcity:** Do NOT use manufactured pressure like *"I only have 1 client spot left this week"* or *"Offer expires in 24 hours"*. Speak from calm confidence.
3. **No Unsolicited Attachments:** Never send unsolicited PDFs, zip files, or heavy mockups in cold DMs or first emails. It triggers spam filters and feels intrusive. Send clean links instead.
4. **Immediate Opt-Out Respect:** If a prospect says "No", "Unsubscribe", or "Not interested", thank them immediately and change their CRM status to `Do Not Contact / Not Interested`. Never argue or send further marketing messages.
5. **No Automated Bot Spam:** Never blast generic automated template scrapers. Every message must pass manual human inspection and contain a verified observation.
6. **Strict Separation of Identities:** Never use personal accounts to send aggressive sales pitches. Always sign off as **ShivamWebDesigns**, directing clients to official studio endpoints (`shivamwebdesigns.devs.li`, `shivamwebdesigns23@gmail.com`).
