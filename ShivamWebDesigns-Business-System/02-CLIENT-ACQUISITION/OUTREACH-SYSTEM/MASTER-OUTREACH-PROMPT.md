# ShivamWebDesigns — Master Client Outreach Framework

This document codifies the operational outreach strategy for **ShivamWebDesigns (SWD)**. Our approach is strictly consultative, research-driven, and highly personalized. We do not engage in automated mass spam or generic marketing blasts.

---

## 1. The Core Outreach Philosophy

- **Consultative Value First:** Every message must reference a verified, specific observation about the prospect's actual digital presence.
- **Truthful & Evidence-Based:** Never claim a website is broken, insecure, or slow without observable data.
- **Zero Pressure:** We invite a discussion; we never demand a sales call or manufacture false scarcity/urgency.
- **Official Identity:** Always represent the studio through official channels (`shivamwebdesigns.devs.li`, `shivamwebdesigns23@gmail.com`).

---

## 4. Personalization Guidelines (What to Observe)

1. **Mobile UX Discrepancies:** *"I noticed your desktop layout looks clean, but on mobile devices the navigation menu overflows and the primary contact button requires extensive horizontal scrolling."*
2. **Typography & Readability:** *"Your brand story is compelling, but the light grey body copy on dark backgrounds creates low contrast that makes reading your core services challenging on mobile viewports."*
3. **Conversion Funnel Friction:** *"I explored your project portfolio, but couldn't locate a clear call-to-action or inquiry intake form at the bottom of case studies to capture interested leads."*
4. **Performance & Page Weight:** *"Your hero banner imagery appears uncompressed, causing noticeable layout shifts and slow initial render times on 4G cellular connections."*
