# ShivamWebDesigns — Studio Introduction & Operating Principles

Welcome to our studio culture. This document outlines how **ShivamWebDesigns (SWD)** operates, our core engineering philosophy, and what makes our client partnerships distinct.

---

## 1. Our Origin & Purpose

ShivamWebDesigns was founded on a simple observation: the modern web has become cluttered with bloated page builders, slow plugins, generic AI slop, and forgettable templates.

We exist to counter this drift. We build **bespoke digital flagships** that pair the timeless authority of Swiss editorial design with high-performance modern engineering. We treat your website not as a digital business card, but as your company's primary corporate asset and visual signature.

---

## 2. Our Non-Negotiable Engineering Standards

- **Sub-Second Performance (100/100 Core Web Vitals):** Every millisecond counts. We engineer websites to load in under 1 second, boosting Google SEO rankings and reducing bounce rates.
- **Zero Framework Bloat:** We write clean, hand-crafted code using modern Next.js, React, and TypeScript. We never use opaque third-party builders that break after 6 months.
- **Fluid Multi-Viewport Responsiveness:** Your site is meticulously calibrated across iPhone, Android, iPad, MacBook, 4K monitors, and ultra-wide screens.
- **100% Client IP Ownership:** You own everything we build. All custom Figma art, source code repositories, and brand exports belong to you unconditionally upon project completion.

---

## 3. The Studio Team Structure

- **Creative Direction & Principal Engineering:** Led personally by Shivam Purohit, overseeing visual architecture, UX flows, and code quality.
- **Specialist Collaborators:** For large-scale projects requiring custom 3D WebGL, sound design, or specialized API backends, we bring in vetted technical specialists managed directly under our studio umbrella.
