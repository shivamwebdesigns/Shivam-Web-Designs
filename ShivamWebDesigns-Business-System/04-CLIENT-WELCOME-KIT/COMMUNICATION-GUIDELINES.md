## 3. How to Provide Actionable Design Feedback

To avoid subjective confusion and ensure feedback leads to swift improvements:
1. **Consolidate Feedback:** Gather all stakeholder thoughts into a single email or document rather than sending piecemeal comments across multiple chats.
2. **Focus on the "Why":** Explain the underlying business reason rather than prescribing technical code fixes (e.g., *"This section feels crowded because our primary audience is older and needs larger text"*, rather than *"Change CSS padding to 20px"*).
3. **Use Direct Screenshots / Loom Videos:** Visual annotations or quick 2-minute screen recordings are immensely helpful and save days of back-and-forth emails.
