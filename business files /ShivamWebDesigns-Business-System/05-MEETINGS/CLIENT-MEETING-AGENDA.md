# ShivamWebDesigns — Client Discovery Meeting Agenda

**Session Type:** Introductory Discovery & Technical Diagnostic  
**Duration:** 25 Minutes  
**Platform:** Google Meet (`shivamwebdesigns23@gmail.com`)  
**Attendees:** Shivam Purohit (SWD) + [Client Name(s), Titles]  

---

## 1. Meeting Objectives
1. Understand the client's core business model, target audience, and commercial growth targets.
2. Diagnose technical, aesthetic, and UX shortcomings in their current web presence.
3. Establish mutual cultural and commercial fit.
4. Agree on high-level scope, budget tiers, and deliverable timeline.

---

## 2. Structured Time Breakdown (25 Minutes)

### [00:00 – 03:00] Part 1: Context & Warm Welcome
- Thank the client for their time; verify audio and video clarity.
- Frame the call: *"Our goal today is to dive deep into your brand's digital requirements and see if ShivamWebDesigns is the right strategic partner to bring your vision to life."*
- Confirm target end time to respect the client's calendar.

### [03:00 – 12:00] Part 2: Strategic Discovery & Diagnosis
- Ask core diagnostic questions from [`DISCOVERY-QUESTIONS.md`](DISCOVERY-QUESTIONS.md).
- Listen actively. Take structured notes on customer pain points, conversion bottlenecks, and current frustrations.
- Uncover the single most important metric for project success (e.g., doubling qualified leads, repositioning into luxury tier).

### [12:00 – 18:00] Part 3: Strategic Recommendations & Portfolio Walkthrough
- Screen share briefly to demonstrate relevant architectural solutions:
  - Demonstrate live case study benchmarks on [shivamwebdesigns.devs.li](https://shivamwebdesigns.devs.li).
  - Walk through how our clean Next.js architecture solves their speed, mobile UI, or conversion challenges.

### [18:00 – 22:00] Part 4: Commercial Scope & Timeline Alignment
- Review target launch deadline and any immovable dates (investor meetings, product launches).
- Align on budget range and expected milestone structure (50% upfront, 50% on completion).

### [22:00 – 25:00] Part 5: Action Items & Wrap-Up
- Summarize agreed scope items.
- Commit to next step: *"I will synthesize our discussion into a formal Project Proposal and itemized Quotation within 24 to 48 hours."*
