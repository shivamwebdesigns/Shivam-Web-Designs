## 3. How to Navigate This Operating System

1. **For Lead Generation & Prospecting:** Jump directly to [`02-CLIENT-ACQUISITION/`](../02-CLIENT-ACQUISITION/) for research checklists and outreach message templates.
2. **When an Inquiry Arrives:** Consult [`03-GOOGLE-WORKSPACE/CLIENT-INQUIRY-FORM.md`](../03-GOOGLE-WORKSPACE/CLIENT-INQUIRY-FORM.md) and [`05-MEETINGS/`](../05-MEETINGS/) to conduct the initial discovery call.
3. **Drafting Proposals & Quotes:** Use [`06-PROPOSALS-AND-QUOTATIONS/`](../06-PROPOSALS-AND-QUOTATIONS/) to structure milestone payments, timelines, and deliverables.
4. **Onboarding a Paid Client:** Issue the [`04-CLIENT-WELCOME-KIT/`](../04-CLIENT-WELCOME-KIT/) alongside the executed agreement in [`07-LEGAL-DOCUMENTS/`](../07-LEGAL-DOCUMENTS/).
5. **Managing & Delivering the Sprint:** Track milestones in [`09-PROJECT-MANAGEMENT/`](../09-PROJECT-MANAGEMENT/) and complete final transfer using [`10-DELIVERY-AND-HANDOVER/`](../10-DELIVERY-AND-HANDOVER/).
