# Studio Strategic Roadmap & Future Improvements

This roadmap documents strategic upgrades planned for **ShivamWebDesigns (SWD)** to scale operational efficiency, client value, and agency profitability.

---

## Phase 1: Near-Term Enhancements (Q1 2027)
- [ ] **Automated Payment Gateway Integration:** Connect Razorpay and Stripe to digital business card and quote links for instant 1-click credit card / UPI deposit payments.
- [ ] **Interactive Client Intake Portal:** Convert `CLIENT-INQUIRY-FORM.md` into an interactive, multi-step web form hosted on `shivamwebdesigns.devs.li/start-project` with auto-save and asset upload.
- [ ] **Lighthouse CI Integration:** Set up GitHub Actions to automatically run Google Lighthouse audits on every pull request, preventing performance regressions.

---

## Phase 2: Medium-Term Expansion (Q2–Q3 2027)
- [ ] **Studio Retainer Program:** Target 5 ongoing monthly care clients to generate predictable, recurring baseline revenue.
- [ ] **Interactive 3D Component Library:** Package bespoke lightweight 3D widgets (like the SWD 3D card) into modular, reusable vanilla code snippets.
- [ ] **Automated Client Reporting Dashboard:** Build a lightweight, passwordless client staging portal showing real-time sprint progress, staging links, and invoice history.

---

## Phase 3: Long-Term Scaling (Post-Majority Transition)
- [ ] **Full Corporate Entity Registration:** Transition from freelance sole proprietorship to a registered corporate entity (LLP / Private Limited) upon founder reaching statutory majority.
- [ ] **Direct Merchant Accounts & Contracts:** Streamline Master Service Agreements for unilateral direct signing without guardian advisory requirements.
- [ ] **Junior Developer / Apprentice Sprints:** Bring on junior frontend contributors to handle routine content migration, freeing lead developer for high-ticket bespoke creative architecture.
