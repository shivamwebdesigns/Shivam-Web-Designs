## 2. Managing Scope Creep

When a client requests a feature or page not included in the original Statement of Work:
1. **Acknowledge Politely:** Validate the idea and explain why it's a valuable suggestion.
2. **Clarify Scope Boundary:** Refer gently to the approved SOW without being confrontational.
3. **Offer Constructive Options:** Either swap out a similar-sized feature from the current scope or provide a quick supplementary quotation.

### Word-for-Word Script for Scope Creep
> *"Hi [Client Name], that’s a fantastic feature idea that will definitely enhance user engagement. Because this wasn't part of our initial project scope and sprint allocation, we have two great paths forward:  
> 1. We can launch the core website as scheduled and implement this as Phase 2 immediately after launch.  
> 2. I can prepare a brief supplementary quote and timeline adjustment right now so we build it inside this sprint.  
> Let me know which approach you prefer!"*

---

## 3. Handling Client Disagreements or Frustration

1. **Stay Calm & Objective:** Separate personal emotion from technical and aesthetic decisions.
2. **Listen Actively:** Identify the underlying business concern (e.g., fear of lost revenue, pressure from stakeholders).
3. **Explain the 'Why':** Frame design decisions in terms of user experience, speed, and conversion data rather than subjective taste.
4. **Offer Concrete Solutions:** Propose 2 clear, viable alternatives that solve the problem while protecting project deadlines.
