# Legal & Contract Review Checklist

This checklist guides **ShivamWebDesigns (SWD)** and prospective clients before finalizing any contractual agreement or statement of work.

---

## 1. Statutory Capacity & Signatory Verification
- [ ] **Founder Age Advisory:** Has the client been informed that the founder/developer is a minor?
- [ ] **Adult Legal Co-Signatory:** Is the official guardian / authorized adult co-signatory named on the Master Service Agreement or statement of work?
- [ ] **Client Authority Check:** Has the client representative confirmed they hold legal authority to bind their organization?
- [ ] **Full Corporate Details:** Are legal company name, registration number/GSTIN/PAN, and registered physical address recorded accurately?

---

## 2. Scope & Technical Specification
- [ ] **Deliverables Defined:** Are all pages, components, animations, and forms clearly listed by name and quantity?
- [ ] **Out-of-Scope Items:** Are exclusions explicitly stated (e.g., copywriting, complex backend databases, paid plugin licenses, ongoing content updates)?
- [ ] **Browser Support:** Is the baseline browser compatibility documented (modern evergreen desktop & mobile browsers)?
- [ ] **Responsive Breakpoints:** Are responsive screen widths acknowledged (Desktop ≥1280px, Tablet 768px, Mobile 375px)?

---

## 3. Financial & Milestone Clarity
- [ ] **Total Cost & Currency:** Is the exact total fee confirmed with currency indicated (INR / USD)?
- [ ] **Milestone Tranches:** Is the payment schedule explicitly broken down (e.g., 50% deposit / 25% design preview / 25% final handover)?
- [ ] **Late Fees & Pauses:** Does the client understand that project development pauses if invoices remain unpaid past seven (7) days?
- [ ] **Extra Work Rates:** Is the hourly / day rate for out-of-scope requests or extra revision rounds stated?

---

## 4. Revisions & Approvals
- [ ] **Revision Limits:** Are revision cycles clearly capped at two (2) rounds per milestone?
- [ ] **Review Windows:** Is the five (5) business day feedback turnaround window noted?
- [ ] **Deemed Approval:** Is the clause regarding automatic deemed approval after seven (7) days of non-response accepted?

---

## 5. Intellectual Property & Confidentiality
- [ ] **IP Transfer Condition:** Is it clearly highlighted that source code and asset IP transfer *only* upon 100% final payment settlement?
- [ ] **Portfolio Rights:** Is SWD granted permission to display the project in portfolio case studies and online showcases?
- [ ] **NDA Status:** If the client requires an NDA, has the mutual NDA been signed prior to disclosing sensitive business plans or credentials?
- [ ] **Third-Party Assets:** Has the client confirmed that all logos, imagery, and copy supplied are licensed and free of copyright infringements?

---

## 6. Pre-Launch & Warranty
- [ ] **Bug-Fix Period:** Is the 14-day post-launch warranty period for objective code defects clearly explained?
- [ ] **Exclusions from Warranty:** Are external hosting downtime, DNS misconfigurations, and post-handover client edits excluded?
- [ ] **Credential Security:** Are all temporary access credentials handed over securely and recommended for password rotation?
