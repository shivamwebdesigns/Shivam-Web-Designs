# ShivamWebDesigns — Follow-Up Sequences & Protocol

Timing and cadence are critical. Outreach messages must never badger prospects. We adhere to a strict **maximum of two follow-ups** before closing the lead.

---

## Cadence Timeline
- **Initial Message (Day 0):** Sent via LinkedIn, Instagram, or Email.
- **Follow-Up 1 (Day 4–5):** Gentle nudge with an added piece of value.
- **Follow-Up 2 / The Polite Close (Day 8–10):** Respectful closing message. Leave the door open without guilt.
- **Post-Day 10:** No further messages. Move lead status to `Unresponsive / Closed`.

---

## Follow-Up 1 (Day 4–5) — LinkedIn / Email

```text
Hi {{First_Name}},

Following up briefly on my note from earlier this week regarding {{Company_Name}}'s web experience.

I know how demanding your schedule is. In case it's helpful, here is a quick case study of how we recently modernized a client platform, reducing load time to under 0.8s while doubling mobile lead inquiries:
https://shivamwebdesigns.devs.li

If you'd like to explore a similar upgrade for {{Company_Name}}, I'd love to connect for 10 minutes. 

Best,
ShivamWebDesigns
shivamwebdesigns23@gmail.com
```

## Follow-Up 2 / The Polite Close (Day 8–10)

```text
Hi {{First_Name}},

I assume improving your website isn't a top priority for {{Company_Name}} right now — completely understand.

I won't reach out further, but if you ever decide to elevate your digital presence or need a reliable web design partner in the future, feel free to keep my digital business card handy:
https://shivamwebdesigns.devs.li/businesscard

Wishing you and {{Company_Name}} continued success!

Best regards,
ShivamWebDesigns
```

## Response Handlers

### When Client Responds with Interest ("Tell me more" / "What are your rates?")
```text
Hi {{First_Name}},

Great to hear from you!

Every project at ShivamWebDesigns is tailored to your specific business goals, scope, and technical requirements rather than using cookie-cutter templates.

To give you an accurate scope, recommendations, and pricing, could you share 2–3 sentences about your primary goals for the new site? Alternatively, we can jump on a quick 15-minute Google Meet discovery call whenever convenient:

• Direct Inquiry Form: https://shivamwebdesigns.devs.li/start-project
• Studio Profile: https://shivamwebdesigns.devs.li

Looking forward to your thoughts!
```

### When Client Responds: "Not interested / We have an in-house team"
```text
Thank you for the quick reply, {{First_Name}}! Completely understood. Glad to hear you have that covered. Wishing you continued momentum with {{Company_Name}}!
```
