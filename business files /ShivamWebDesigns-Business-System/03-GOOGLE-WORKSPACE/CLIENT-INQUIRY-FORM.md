# ShivamWebDesigns — Client Inquiry Form Blueprint

Use this specification to create your public client project intake form in Google Forms or on your website.

---

## Form Title: Project Inquiry & Discovery Brief — ShivamWebDesigns
**Form Description:**  
*We craft bespoke digital flagships, high-performance websites, and considered digital experiences. Complete this brief to help us understand your vision, requirements, and timeline. All inquiries receive a personal response within 24 hours.*

---

### Section 1: Contact & Company Profile
1. **Full Name**  
   - Type: Short answer | Required: Yes
2. **Business / Brand Name**  
   - Type: Short answer | Required: Yes
3. **Official Email Address**  
   - Type: Short answer | Required: Yes | Validation: Email pattern
4. **Phone / WhatsApp Number (with Country Code)**  
   - Type: Short answer | Required: Yes
5. **Existing Website URL (if any)**  
   - Type: Short answer | Required: No | Help text: *Leave blank if this is a brand new project.*
6. **Company Location & Time Zone**  
   - Type: Short answer | Required: Yes

---

### Section 2: Project Scope & Objectives
7. **What service(s) are you seeking?**  
   - Type: Checkboxes | Required: Yes
   - [ ] New Website Design & Development (from scratch)
   - [ ] Website Redesign & Modernization
   - [ ] UI/UX Design & Prototyping
   - [ ] Spatial 3D / Interactive Experience
   - [ ] Performance Optimization (Sub-second Core Web Vitals)
   - [ ] Ongoing Retainer & Maintenance
8. **What are the primary business goals of this project?**  
   - Type: Paragraph | Required: Yes
   - Help text: *e.g., Increase sales conversion, elevate brand perception, launch new product line, recruit top talent.*
9. **Who is your primary target audience?**  
   - Type: Paragraph | Required: Yes

---

### Section 3: Budget Tier & Timeline
10. **Estimated Project Investment Budget**  
    - Type: Multiple choice | Required: Yes
    - ( ) Tier 1: Sprint Flagship ($1,000 – $2,500 / ₹80,000 – ₹2,00,000)
    - ( ) Tier 2: Comprehensive Studio Build ($2,500 – $5,000 / ₹2,00,000 – ₹4,00,000)
    - ( ) Tier 3: Enterprise / Custom Interactive ($5,000+ / ₹4,00,000+)
    - ( ) Flexible / Seeking Professional Recommendation
11. **Desired Target Launch Date**  
    - Type: Multiple choice | Required: Yes
    - ( ) Urgent (within 2–3 weeks)
    - ( ) Standard (4–6 weeks)
    - ( ) Long-term (2–3 months)
    - ( ) Flexible based on design excellence

---

### Section 4: Visual References & Additional Details
12. **Are there 2–3 websites whose design, structure, or interactions you admire?**  
    - Type: Paragraph | Required: No | Help text: *Please include URLs and a short note on what you like.*
13. **Any specific technical requirements or integrations?**  
    - Type: Paragraph | Required: No | Help text: *e.g., CRM integration, newsletter signup, custom animations, multi-language.*
