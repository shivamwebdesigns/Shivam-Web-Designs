# ShivamWebDesigns — Client Onboarding Guide

To keep your project on schedule and ensure a fluid design process, please complete the four steps below during your onboarding week.

---

## Step 1: Complete the Onboarding Form (Est. 10 Minutes)
Please complete our official onboarding form at:  
👉 `[Google Onboarding Form Link / Dedicated Intake Link]`  
This provides our team with your primary stakeholders, design approvals contact, and target launch objectives.

---

## Step 2: Upload Brand & Content Assets to Shared Drive
We will create a private Google Drive folder for your company: `SWD - [Your Company Name]`. Please upload:
1. **Brand Logos:** Vector format (`.SVG`, `.EPS`, or high-res transparent `.PNG`). Include dark and light variants.
2. **Typography Files:** Font files (`.TTF`, `.WOFF2`) or links to Google / Adobe Fonts.
3. **Imagery & Photography:** High-resolution uncompressed photos, team portraits, product images, or office photography.
4. **Copy & Text:** Final written copy for each page in Google Docs or Word format. *(Note: If you require copy structuring assistance, let us know early).*

---

## Step 3: Grant Technical Access
When you are ready, please grant access to the following systems so we can prepare your staging environment:
- **Domain Registrar:** Invite `shivamwebdesigns23@gmail.com` as a delegated technical manager (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare, etc.) or prepare to update DNS A/CNAME records at launch.
- **Third-Party Services:** Google Analytics 4, Resend/SendGrid API keys (if applicable).

---

## Step 4: Schedule Your Sprint Kickoff Call
Book your 30-minute kickoff session via our studio link:  
👉 `[Google Meet / Scheduling Link]`  
During this call, we will review your assets, align on interactive design references, and confirm the milestone schedule.
