LEGAL & COMMERCIAL ENGAGEMENT TERMS

Terms of Service & Engagement

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 25, 2026 • ShivamWebDesigns

Engagement Philosophy: We partner with ambitious brands to build exceptional, high-performance web experiences. Our contracts are built on clear milestones, zero ambiguity, full intellectual property transfer upon final settlement, and guaranteed post-launch stability.

1. Scope of Services

ShivamWebDesigns provides bespoke digital design, UI/UX architecture, frontend engineering, WebGL / 3D spatial development, and technical consulting. Each client engagement is defined by an approved Statement of Work (SOW) detailing timeline, deliverables, technical stack, and milestone investment.

2. Intellectual Property Rights

Upon receipt of full and final payment for an engagement, all custom design assets, frontend code, 3D models specifically created for the project, and proprietary client brand materials transfer 100% to the client. ShivamWebDesigns retains the right to display the completed work in its portfolio, case studies, and industry design awards unless an explicit non-disclosure agreement (NDA) prohibits public presentation.

3. Milestone Schedule & Invoicing

Engagements are structured under milestone-based gates (typically 50% deposit upon kickoff, 25% upon design approval, and 25% prior to production code deployment). Invoices are payable within 14 days of presentation. Milestone sign-offs ensure continuous alignment before moving to subsequent development phases.

4. Post-Launch Warranty

Every flagship build includes a 30-day post-launch warranty period at zero extra charge. During this window, any defects, browser rendering regressions, or performance bottlenecks identified against the approved specifications are remediated immediately.

5. Client Responsibilities

Clients agree to provide brand guidelines, copy, media assets, and timely feedback on milestone reviews within reasonable timeframes (typically 3 business days) to prevent project timeline slippage.
