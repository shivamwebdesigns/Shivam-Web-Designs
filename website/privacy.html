LEGAL & COMPLIANCE // DPDP ACT & GDPR

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 25, 2026 • ShivamWebDesigns

Privacy Summary: We believe in zero slop and zero unnecessary surveillance. We do not sell your personal information, we do not use invasive third-party advertising cookies, and we only collect data you actively provide when submitting a project brief or contacting us.

1. Information We Collect

ShivamWebDesigns collects only the minimal information required to deliver high-quality digital design, 3D web engineering, and client communication:

  • Voluntary Contact Information: Name, email address, company name, project brief details, budget and timeline selections, and contact message contents submitted through our forms or sent directly to us.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: Standard server access logs (IP address, browser user-agent, operating system) collected automatically by our hosting platform (Vercel) for DDoS protection, traffic security, and uptime monitoring.
  • No Tracking Cookies: We do not deploy advertising tracking pixels, cross-site identity trackers, or third-party behavioral cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly used to:

  • Review, scope, and formulate proposals for your website or 3D web application.
  • Communicate with you regarding active contracts, milestones, and deliverables.
  • Maintain the security, integrity, and performance of our web infrastructure.

When you submit a project brief or contact message, its details are stored in our secure Firebase Firestore database so the studio can review and respond. If email notifications are configured, a copy is routed through Resend under strict encryption.

3. Legal Bases for Processing & DPDP Act Compliance

We process personal data in compliance with applicable global data privacy regulations, including the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 of India and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) standards. Our processing relies on:

  • Consent: When you voluntarily submit a project inquiry or contact us.
  • Contractual Necessity: To fulfill design and development obligations for client engagements.
  • Legitimate Interests: To ensure server security, prevent abusive bot requests, and maintain reliable service availability.

4. Data Retention & Storage

Inquiry records in Firestore and any notification emails are retained only as long as needed to review and respond, maintain a client relationship, or meet legal and accounting obligations. Stored data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections or updates, or request complete deletion of your contact records. To exercise any of these rights, email us directly.
