06 / ANSWERS

Straight Answers

Frequently Discussed Topics
Who will actually be designing and coding my project?

Shivam leads and personally executes every critical phase of design, information architecture, and frontend engineering. You work directly with senior craftspeople, not junior account managers or offshore subcontractors.

How do you guarantee sub-second page performance?

By building with modern web platform primitives: semantic HTML, native CSS layouts (Grid and Container Queries), strict image format pipelines (AVIF/WebP), tree-shaken JavaScript, and zero unnecessary npm bloatware. We test every build against real-world mobile throttling before shipping.

Can my marketing team easily add new pages and edit content?

Yes. We pair our websites with intuitive, visual headless CMS setups like Sanity or Strapi. You receive a custom schema tailored specifically to your content blocks, complete with recorded video guides for your internal team.

What are your billing milestones and payment terms?

For fixed projects, our standard model is 50% deposit upon project agreement and 50% on final staging QA approval prior to production release. We accept wire transfer, Stripe, and direct international invoice payments.

Do you provide maintenance and ongoing updates after launch?

Every project includes 30 days of active post-launch bug fixing and monitoring. Beyond that, clients can transition into our monthly Studio Retainer for continuous improvements, new landing pages, and ongoing CRO experiments.
